      Description
      The MSUSBLC6-2SC6 are monolithic application specific devices dedicated to ESD protection of high speed
       interfaces, such as USB 2.0, Ethernet links and video lines.The very low line capacitance secures a high le
      vel of signal integrity without compromising in protecting sensitive chips against the most stringently characteri
      zed ESD strikes.
